The challenge provided a photograph of a fish dish served on a banana leaf and described a 
restaurant in Kerala with green paddy fields across the road. 
Investigation 
The phrase “God’s Own Country” pointed to Kerala. The photograph showed Karimeen 
Pollichathu, a fish preparation cooked in a banana leaf. 
A reverse-image search returned similar dishes but did not locate an exact copy of the 
challenge photograph. Restaurant galleries were then compared using the presentation of 
the fish, white plates, banana leaves and stone tabletops as visual clues.
