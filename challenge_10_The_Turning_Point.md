Overview 
The challenge describes a major sporting event that ended in disappointment for one team. 
After the match, one of the team’s most famous players announced what appeared to be his 
retirement from international football. The decision later changed, making the episode a 
clear “turning point” in his career. 
The clues point to Lionel Messi and Argentina’s defeat in the 2016 Copa América Centenario 
final.

Lionel Messi missed Argentina’s first penalty. After the defeat, Messi announced that he was

2. The Retirement Statement 
Following the final, Messi spoke to reporters and said that he was ending his international 
career with Argentina. 
The announcement attracted worldwide attention because Argentina had lost several major 
finals during that period, including: 
• 
The 2014 FIFA World Cup final 
• 
The 2015 Copa América final 
• 
The 2016 Copa América Centenario final 
The 2016 final was especially significant because Messi missed a penalty during the shootout 
and then announced his retirement shortly afterward. 
FIFA’s report described the announcement as taking place after Argentina’s penalty-
shootout defeat to Chile at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey. 
3. Confirming That the Decision Was Reversed 
The retirement announcement was not permanent. 
On 12 August 2016, Messi reversed his decision and confirmed that he would continue 
playing for Argentina.
